Why Use the Free Hit in FPL GW3?
The biggest advantage of the Free Hit chip is flexibility.
A normal FPL transfer has to be considered in the context of future Gameweeks. If you bring in a player for GW3, you generally need to think about whether you will still want that player in GW4, GW5 and beyond.
The Free Hit removes much of that problem.
You can target a favourable fixture for GW3 without worrying about having to keep the player afterwards.
This makes the chip particularly useful when there is a significant difference between the fixtures you want to target for one Gameweek and the players you want for the weeks that follow.
Key Decisions for the FPL GW3 Free Hit
1. Who Should You Captain?
Captaincy is arguably the most important decision in any Free Hit team.
A strong captain can make a huge difference because every point scored by your captain is doubled.
For GW3, I would prioritise players who combine:
Strong fixture + attacking role + expected minutes + penalty/set-piece potential + high goal involvement.
The ideal Free Hit captain isn't necessarily the most expensive player. It is the player with the best combination of fixture and expected points.
2. Which Premium Players Are Worth Paying For?
A Free Hit can also provide an opportunity to concentrate money into premium players with particularly attractive fixtures.
However, simply selecting the most expensive players isn't necessarily the optimal strategy.
The question should be:
Which players have the highest expected points relative to their price for Gameweek 3?
That can create opportunities to pair one or two premium picks with cheaper players who have strong fixtures or attacking potential.
3. Finding the Best FPL Differentials
One of the biggest attractions of a Free Hit is the opportunity to attack with players that aren't widely owned.
A differential doesn't have to be an obscure £4.5m player.
A player with moderate ownership who has an excellent GW3 fixture can potentially become a major rank booster if they return while highly-owned alternatives blank.
The key is balancing upside and risk.
You don't want an entire Free Hit squad full of differentials. A better approach is normally to combine reliable high-owned players with a smaller number of carefully selected differentials.
Best FPL GW3 Strategy
When building a Free Hit team, I would work through the squad in this order:
Step 1 — Identify the best fixtures
Start with the Gameweek 3 fixture list rather than individual players.
Step 2 — Find the best attacking teams
Look for teams with strong attacking potential and favourable opposition.
Step 3 — Select your captain
Choose your captain before filling the rest of the squad.
Step 4 — Build your premium core
Select the players you believe have the highest ceiling.
Step 5 — Add value picks
Use cheaper players who offer strong expected points, minutes or fixture potential.
Step 6 — Add differentials
Look for lower-owned players who could make a significant difference to your rank.
Step 7 — Check expected minutes
A brilliant fixture isn't much use if the player is unlikely to play enough minutes.
Step 8 — Review the bench
Don't waste money unnecessarily, but make sure your bench provides sufficient cover.
Step 9 — Reassess before the deadline
Team news, injuries, press conferences and predicted line-ups can completely change a Free Hit decision.
